===== WHAT DID YOU BUILD? (copy everything between the lines) =====
------------------------------------------------------------------
Canon is the rights layer for creative IP on NEAR. Every work is a NEP-171 NFT that owns its own keyless vault: a sub-account with zero access keys running a 3.5 KB vault published as an immutable NEP-591 global contract. Derivatives join a work's lineage only when the parent's current owner approves them on-chain. A studio gets one clearance verdict across every ancestor, then a single buy_license transaction splits the payment up the whole family tree: each work's vault and every contributor is paid, and the studio receives a licence NFT that unlocks the source files. Resales pay royalties to every ancestor through NEP-199. Control follows the token: a seller cannot withdraw after a sale, cannot sell mid-withdrawal, and cannot drain a listed vault. The registry has no admin, pause or upgrade method, and its keys are deleted. Proof: 41 contract tests plus 41 real testnet transactions, including 11 refused attacks, all linked in LEDGER.md and on the Proof page. Live app: https://canon.bootstate.xyz. Contract: canonhq.testnet.
